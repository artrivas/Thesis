## Hero figure 1 -- divergence (strongest "no universal metric" evidence)

**Cell:** `barabasi_albert` x `triangle_insertion`.

Recomputed Spearman sensitivity (alpha vs. distribution_score, pooled over 24
seeds x 11 alphas):

| Workflow | Sensitivity (rho) | Monotonicity violation | Seed CV |
|---|---|---|---|
| GraphStats+MMD | **-0.048** | **0.70** (7/10 alpha-steps decrease) | 0.021 |
| WL+MMD | 0.996 | 0.00 | 0.022 |
| NetLSD | 0.996 | 0.00 | 0.014 |
| Diversity Curves | 0.996 | 0.00 | 0.012 |

This is not "GraphStats+MMD saturates early" (the framing used in the existing
`poster_figures/panel_captions.md` panel B) -- the per-alpha mean curve
(`analyze2.py` / raw CSV) actually rises to a peak at alpha~=0.2-0.3 and then
**declines monotonically** all the way to alpha=1.0, ending up lower than at
alpha=0.2. That is a genuine sign reversal: at high perturbation the metric
would rank a heavily-perturbed graph as *more* similar to the original than a
moderately-perturbed one. The same inverted-U/breakdown appears in `erdos_renyi`
(rho=-0.018) and `stochastic_block_model` (rho=0.072, still near zero) for the
same perturbation, i.e. it is a systematic GraphStats+MMD failure mode for
triangle insertion on synthetic graphs, not single-cell noise. It does **not**
reproduce on `imdb_binary` triangle_insertion (rho=0.97 there) -- worth a footnote
if space allows, since it shows the failure is tied to the synthetic generative
structure, not universal even for GraphStats+MMD.

**Caption used on the figure:** "GraphStats+MMD REVERSES (rho = -0.05) while the
other three keep climbing (rho ~ 0.99) as more triangles are inserted."

## Hero figure 2 -- cleanest agreement ("textbook" case)

**Cell:** `erdos_renyi` x `edge_deletion` (marginally the cleanest of the four
edge_deletion cells; SBM is essentially tied and BA/IMDB slightly noisier):

| Dataset | sens_min | sens_range | mono_max | CV_max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ER** | **0.9897** | **0.0065** | 0.0 | **0.034** |
| SBM | 0.9847 | 0.0115 | 0.0 | 0.037 |
| BA | 0.9525 | 0.0437 | 0.0 | 0.011 |
| IMDB | 0.9906 | 0.0058 | 0.0 | 0.160 |

All four workflows are perfectly monotonic (violation = 0) and rho >= 0.99 on
ER edge_deletion, with seed CV <= 3.4%. This is quantitatively the single
lowest-noise, most-agreed-upon cell in the whole 96-cell grid.

**Caveat corrected vs. an early draft of this figure:** the four *normalized*
curves do **not** visually overlap -- GraphStats+MMD and Diversity Curves rise
early, while NetLSD and (especially) WL+MMD stay near zero until alpha>0.8 then
shoot up. Each curve is independently monotonic and low-noise (that's the real
"textbook" property), but their sensitivity *onset* differs sharply. The figure
caption says exactly this ("each reliably, though their sensitivity onset
differs") rather than the inaccurate "overlap tightly" phrasing from an earlier
draft -- don't claim curve-shape agreement, only rank/monotonicity agreement.
